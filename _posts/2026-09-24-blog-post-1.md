Fruit and Vegetable Study by UCONN

I saw a Facebook post about "Fruit and Vegetable Study" by UCONN.  They are looking for volunteers to record a week of food and drink, then go to UCONN to have a body scan and test out some sensors.  I have been wondering my food intake for a while and see no any harm, also wanted to do a body scan for a while, plus $300 incentive, I jumped in.
