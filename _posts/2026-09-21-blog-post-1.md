I started to develop Android app at the very beginning of the birth of app store.  When I released "Speed Dial" app, there were only about 20 or so apps in the app store.  People were really looking for app.  I remembered that I just released the app, some comments alrealy poped up saying not working correctly.  The "Speed Dial" was a very popular app at the time.  It allows user to create a contact image on the home screen. Just tap the image, it will start to call the contact. It was really convienent feature and people loved it.  When the app store allow the paid app, I released it for 99 cents app and it sell well.  Eventually, the feature got build into the phone and the sale dwindled away.

Here is another funning story of app sale.  Orignally the store only sell app in US, and more and more countries were added in, so to set up the prices for all my app for many countries was tedious and time consuming. You had to convert the currency to the correspondand country and filled out the price manually (later they filled out automatically based on US price). I had a Facebook "My Friends" app and it would let you do many things the current official Facebook app does, but at that time, the official Facebook app had not released yet.  When I set the price, I was thinking to set it to 99 cents but somehow I forgot the decimal, instead of 0.99, I set it to 99, dollars.  Few days later, from the sale dashboard, I noticed my sale just to hundreds of dollars, instead of usually less than a hundred. People were really hungary at the phone app and would pay any price for it. Eventually I found the cause and changed it back, and refund some of them.

At the peak of the app sale, I made over a thousand dollars a month and it continued for several years.  I was really thinking that it would last until the my retirement year.  But it died down quickly, to a few hundred dollars a year.  then a dozen or so, enventually it became zero.

So long! my Android apps!
